Start from photos of your room

Take one photo of each wall, standing back as far as you can. Mark the wall's four corners and its windows and doors, and the planner works out an empty 3D room. Two walls that meet at a corner give you the full room.

Import a room scan

On a phone with LiDAR, scan the room with an app such as Polycam, 3D Scanner App or Scaniverse. Export as GLB (or OBJ / USDZ) and import it here. The scan is placed on the floor and you can set the room size from it.

Model furniture from a photo

Take a photo straight from the front. Fit the box around the piece, say what it is and type one real measurement, such as the width from the shop or a tape measure. The height and colors come from the photo.

Import a 3D scan of a piece

For an exact copy, scan the piece with Polycam, Scaniverse or Apple Object Capture, export GLB / OBJ / USDZ, and import it. Scans in metres come in at true size; you can rescale in Edit.

Build from a shopping link

Paste a product link, or the whole share text from an app (Taobao: Share → Copy link). The planner reads the size and the kind of furniture from the link and its title, e.g. "200*90*85cm" or "长180 宽80 高75". If the title has no size, paste the size from the product page below. Shops don't let other websites open their pages, so it can't look inside the page itself.

Room from photos

  1. Pick the wall, then choose a photo of it. Stand back so you see the floor line and the ceiling line.
  2. Drag the four dots onto the wall's corners: ceiling-left, ceiling-right, floor-right, floor-left. Corners hidden behind furniture or out of the photo can go outside the picture.
  3. Add each window and door, and drag its two dots onto its corners.
  4. Type one measurement you know, then apply. Do the next wall the same way.
